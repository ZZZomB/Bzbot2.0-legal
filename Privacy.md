### BzBot 2.0 Privacy Policy
We care about user privacy and do everything possible to respect user's information and comply with any regulations about that information. 

The policy below explains what data the **BzBot2.0** Discord bot stores, why, and how to
get it deleted. By using BzBot2.0, or by being a member of a server it has been added to, you agree
to this policy. If you do not agree, do not use the bot's commands.

**Last updated:** 9 October 2026

## Information we Collect
Information we collect includes basic discord account data, voluntarily provided information you choose to give the bot, and interaction data.
**Basic Discord Account Data**
If you are in a server with our bot, the bot may know your basic account information such as your username, profile picture, account age, join date, etc. Basic account data is all publicly available data.
**Voluntarily Provided Data**
Voluntary data is any data you knowingly choose to enter into an event, contest, art contest, info tags, poll, etc. It is when you click something on the bot to submit data to us. All submissions are stored and copied to protect them from being lost during discord issues, but voluntarily provided data is only used for the purpose you enter it for and nothing more.
**Interaction Data**
If you interact with the bot, it may record information about commands ran, event participation, the server ID where a command was used, and information about any changes you made to content (such as the `/info` command). 

## Use of Data
We do not use any data users provide for anything outside of Discord and the bot's features. Basic account data is used to limit who may enter events, for moderation tools, and for banning users from using the bot. Voluntarily provided data is used only for the clear purpose it is submitted for and nowhere else. Interaction data is only used for moderation or to create general usage stats without identifiable data to see what users are using in order to plan new features and updates. We do not use your data for advertising or profit.

## Who we share it with
Your data is not sold, rented, or shared with any third party except our service providers involved in enabling your access to the bot, including data storage, hosting and server providers, error loggers, maintenance, and IT service providers. Content
you submit is also visible inside the Discord server you submitted it in, for
example, server staff can see application answers, and approved contest entries
are posted publicly in that server.

## Data Deletion
Basic account data gathered for moderation is maintained for 30 days after you leave a server and then deleted except for user ID and username, which may be kept as part of the logs below.
Voluntarily submitted data for contests, polls, and apps are deleted from the bot's databases within 90 days of the submission source closing. 
Voluntarily submitted data for info tags is maintained until the user asks for their user ID, name, and information to be removed or until the guide becomes too outdated to continue to use.
All interaction data is stored only as a generic stat (X people viewed this info tag) or is deleted within 90 days.
Ban records are not deleted to protect the integrity of the bot, but only keep minimal data to maintain the ban.

## Your Rights to Delete Data
If you wish to delete your data, please email zzzombee01@gmail.com with your discord user ID and the server IDs the data is connected to. We will respond within 30 days confirming data deletion. Data deletion is completely free and no reason needs to be provided. 

When you delete data, any active submissions you have submitted for contests or applications will be removed, and you will be prevented from entering contests and events for a short time to prevent using data deletion to abuse the entry system.

### What we cannot delete
**Ban records.** - If these could be erased on request, anyone could remove their
  own ban. We keep them so servers stay moderated.
**Submitted content on Discord** - When you submit, content is often posted in a channel for staff to review or users to vote on. We cannot delete every submission posted on Discord, but that submission will no longer be editable and all data for it within the bot will be removed.

## Age
Discord requires all users to be at least 13, or older where local law sets a
higher age. We do not knowingly store data from anyone below that age. If you
believe we have, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes
If we change this policy we will update the date at the top.
